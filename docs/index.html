TubeCheck

Paste a 4130 size. See whether it actually meets 2026 Size A, B, C, or D on all four minima — inertia, area, OD, and wall — then nest cut lengths onto 12 ft or 20 ft sticks.

This is not Formula SAE, not SAE International, and not a substitute for the official Structural Equivalency Spreadsheet. A tube that is both minimum OD and minimum wall may still fail I and area. Verify every number before you cut or submit.
