# 4130 Team Pack

Unofficial helper for Formula SAE 2026 steel tube sizing. Not SAE. Not SES.

Includes:

- Full 4130 catalog mapped to 2026 Size A/B/C/D (`4130-catalog.csv`)
- Reminder: 1.00×0.083 is not Size A. 1.375×0.049 fails Size D on OD if you convert inches strictly (34.925 mm vs 35.0 mm).

Cut lists from TubeCheck are greedy nests, not a laser optimizer. Verify every number before you cut or submit.
