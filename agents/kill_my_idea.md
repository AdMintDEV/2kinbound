# Kill My Idea — output contract

Input: opportunity or live product brief.  
Output: `experiments/active/<slug>_KILL.md` with:

- Claim under test
- Strongest evidence FOR / AGAINST
- Verdict: SURVIVES | PIVOT | KILL
- What would change the verdict
- Recommended next experiment (or stop)

No third product until TubeCheck and Inbound Score each have a written verdict.
